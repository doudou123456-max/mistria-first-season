Quick answers

  1. Chest near home is craft priority one.
  2. Wood and stone feed quests and repairs.
  3. Visit carpenter / workbench when you need blueprints.
  4. Furniture sets can wait.
  5. Keep materials for donation boxes.

Essentials list

  1. Storage
  2. Materials stockpile
  3. Quest turn-ins (wood/stone)
  4. Then nice furniture

Carpenter loop

Ryis and Landen on the Eastern Road / town workbench area handle building progress. Bring wood when requests ask—do not confuse the boarded bell tower for their shop.

Safe to skip

  • Decorating every empty tile
  • Crafting while crops wilt
