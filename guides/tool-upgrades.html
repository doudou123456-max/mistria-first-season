Quick answers

  1. Grab free worn tools from quests before paying.
  2. Watering and clearing pain usually beat combat FOMO.
  3. Blacksmith trips need materials and gold—plan downtime.
  4. One upgrade at a time.
  5. Confirm prices in your shop UI.

Free before paid

Request board and NPC quests handed us early tools (worn pickaxe path, bug net). Check Olric / board before emptying the wallet at the forge.

Pain-first order

  1. Whatever makes watering or clearing miserable
  2. Pickaxe when mines are actually open
  3. Nice-to-have combat last

Safe to skip

  • Max tier before Summer
  • Buying a tool you will not use this week
