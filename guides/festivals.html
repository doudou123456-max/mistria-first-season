Quick answers

  1. Water before you dress up for town.
  2. Talk, watch, leave with energy left.
  3. Missing a prize is not a restart.
  4. Friday inn gatherings are social, not mandatory homework.
  5. Read mail so you know which day to show up.

How we attended

We showed up, talked to a few people, and went home. No spreadsheet of festival min-max.

Inn nights

Sleeping Dragon Inn dinners are cozy. Go when crops are safe. Josephine and friends can wait a week.

Safe to skip

  • Winning every minigame
  • Romance deadlines at first festivals
