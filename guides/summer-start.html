Quick answers

  1. Dead spring crops will not revive—scythe and replant.
  2. Protect gold for summer seeds late spring.
  3. Manor garden roses appear in summer.
  4. Beach and festivals after the patch is alive.
  5. Same watering rule as spring.

Season flip ops

  1. Clear brown spring plants
  2. Shop summer seeds (not on a wasted closed day)
  3. Plant a finishable patch
  4. Then roses / beach / mines

Roses note

East manor garden bushes by the gazebo. Useful for Elsie’s rose requests. Do not confuse with planted flower beds.

Safe to skip

  • Giant summer field day one
  • Spending the last seed coin at a stall
