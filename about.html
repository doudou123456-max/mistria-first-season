About Mistria First Season

Unofficial fan site · Not affiliated with NPC Studio

Mistria First Season is a free, unofficial Fields of Mistria guide site focused on first-season checklists, calm priorities, and “what to skip” advice for new farmers.

Who operates this site

This is an independent fan project. We publish practical English guides for beginners. We are not a studio, retailer, or official publisher. Contact: see the Contact page.

Editorial approach

  • Guides prioritize decisions and routines over wiki dumps.
  • Published pages are human-edited for clarity; we prefer priorities over fabricated drop rates.
  • We use real game screenshots as we play.
  • We update pages when major game updates change early advice.

Independence & trademarks

Fan-made guide site — not affiliated with NPC Studio. Fields of Mistria and related names are trademarks of their respective owners. Mistria First Season is not endorsed by or sponsored by the rights holders.

Advertising
